# Reference and Parity Scope

This publication candidate intentionally contains no Nightingale, Museeks, Cog,
or Swinsian application/reference trees and imports no history from them.
The local development checkout remains separate and unchanged.

Current native Swift/C source, tests, explicit product requirements, and approved
tasks define behavior. Historical Songbird/Nightingale ideas informed names and
visual vocabulary, but they do not establish source/asset permission or an ongoing
pixel-identical requirement. Do not copy reference implementations. The owner-approved restoration of the
existing runtime bird images is recorded in publication/artwork-decision.json.

The owner approved retaining the existing bird artwork for the hobby build. Copied bytes, recoloring, a renderer, or an inspired-by
statement do not establish independent creation or trademark clearance.
See [the licensing review](docs/LICENSING_REVIEW.md) and [baseline boundaries](docs/PUBLICATION_BASELINE.md).

Any future reference investigation must be narrowly scoped and read-only, and any
reuse must follow the explicit owner scope and retain accurate provenance/notices. Missing excluded references
are intentional, not a reason to restore them into this repository.
